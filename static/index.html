AI Assistant — draft parameters & notes from any file

Upload a PIF, PPD, or EST file (xlsx, pdf, image, or text). The AI drafts building parameters and a short engineering summary. Requires ANTHROPIC_API_KEY in your local .env file — everything else in this app works without it. Output is always a DRAFT for your engineer to review.

How this works

1. Weight prediction. Inverse-distance-weighted nearest neighbours over your historical projects (width, eave height, wind speed, live load, enclosure) predict a kg/m² rate; multiplied by area for the base weight. With no history, a rule-of-thumb formula is used instead.

2. Special items. Mezzanine, crane and canopy weight are added using simple per-unit rates you can edit — not derived from load analysis.

3. Category split. Base weight is divided into Built-up / Deep-section / Secondary members / Purlins & girts / Sheeting / Accessories using your percentages.

4. Drawing preview. A schematic anchor-bolt grid and cross-section from your basic dimensions, for visual sanity-checking only.

What this deliberately does not do

  • No wind/seismic load-combination analysis or joint reactions
  • No member sizing, deflection checks, or connection design
  • No DWG parsing
  • No generation of certified/stamped drawings

Everything here is meant to speed up early budgeting before your engineering team runs the real structural design in your normal software, and before a licensed engineer reviews and approves it.
